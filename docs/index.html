Czech initiative helps secure a two-year postponement of methane obligations for oil and gas importers
Česká iniciativa pomohla prosadit dvouletý odklad metanových povinností pro dovozce plynu a ropy

A Czech-led diplomatic initiative backed by 20 EU member states secured a provisional agreement with the European Parliament and Commission to delay key obligations under the EU Methane Regulation for gas and oil importers by two years. Minister of Industry and Trade Karel Havlíček justified the postponement by citing the need to safeguard energy security and shield industry and households from rising energy costs.
